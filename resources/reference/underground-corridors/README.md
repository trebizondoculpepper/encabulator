# Underground corridor references

The 24 original photographs in this directory were copied from `/Users/salimfadhley/Downloads/Underground Corridors` on 16 October 2026 at the owner's request. They are source references for dev7 and are not loaded by the game.

The model draws on their white painted blockwork, concrete floors, dark skirting, exposed ducts and cable trays, wall mounted fluorescent lights, grey fire doors, coloured diagonal wall graphics, service equipment, blue lockers and lift lobby. The corridor route and adjoining rooms are a composite layout rather than a measured reconstruction of the photographed building.
