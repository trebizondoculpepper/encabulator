# Door recording sources

Joseph Sardin: [Creaking metallic door, sound 0616](https://bigsoundbank.com/creaking-metallic-door-s0616.html), CC0. Original downloaded from https://bigsoundbank.com/UPLOAD/bwf-en/0616.wav and retained locally as `0616-metal-door-original.wav` (working WAVs are ignored by git).

rubberduck: [100 CC0 metal and wood SFX](https://opengameart.org/content/100-cc0-metal-and-wood-sfx), CC0. `rubberduck-door-open-01.ogg` is the unchanged `door_open_01.ogg` from https://opengameart.org/sites/default/files/100-CC0-wood-metal-SFX.zip.

`cuts.csv` records the ten distinct game movement cues and source times. Prepared assets and processing notes are in `src/audio/assets/README.md`; attribution is in `CREDITS.md`.
