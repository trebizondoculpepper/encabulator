# Shopping-cart recording

“Noisy Rolling Metal Cart on Rubber Wheels” by [reconsider59](https://freesound.org/people/reconsider59/), published 21 July 2017. [Original source page](https://freesound.org/people/reconsider59/sounds/397994/). The author describes a wire metal shopping cart noisily rolling; its tags include squeak and wheels. The source page states [CC0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/).

`noisy-rolling-metal-cart-original.mp3` is the public [HQ MP3 preview](https://cdn.freesound.org/previews/397/397994_5388550-hq.mp3), downloaded unchanged. It is 20.801 seconds of stereo 44.1 kHz audio. The separately downloadable original WAV requires a Freesound login and was not used.

The local working `filtered.wav` and delivered `src/audio/assets/trolley-squeak.wav` use 3.65–11.85 seconds. The preparation, levels and loop crossfade are recorded in `src/audio/assets/README.md`; the central author and licence credit is in `CREDITS.md`.
