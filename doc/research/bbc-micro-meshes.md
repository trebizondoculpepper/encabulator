# BBC Micro replacement mesh research

Researched 18 October 2026. [VirtualBeeb](https://virtual.bbcmic.ro/) credits Ant Mercer for its BBC model. [Dominic Pajak's project history](https://www.dompajak.com/blog/webxr-beeb-virtual-beeb/index.html) describes obtaining permission and exporting the original Blender geometry to GLTF for Three.js. The [preview](https://www.dompajak.com/blog/webxr-beeb-virtual-beeb/model.png) shows a sculpted case/keyboard and rounded Cub tube/bezel. The public 4 MB GLB has embedded textures and both computer and monitor. It is downloaded and displayed beside the original workstation in the local Dev19 room. Creator, source, stated website terms and unresolved separate model rights are recorded in CREDITS.md and the model-folder README. Production keeps the original model pending rights confirmation.

A commercial alternative is [3DModels.org's Acorn BBC Micro (1981)](https://3dmodels.org/3d-models/acorn-bbc-micro-1981/), with Blender, GLB/glTF, FBX and OBJ formats and a royalty-free listing. Its indexed [Norwegian page](https://3dmodels.org/no/3d-models/acorn-bbc-micro-1981/) showed approximately £45–£50, with optimization services sold separately. Direct page retrieval was blocked; checkout price, licence tier, polygon count and texture details remain unverified. No purchase has been made. A budget question was presented to the owner; absent approval, proceed with the freely downloadable comparison only.

Dev19 is linked between Building Fittings and Rhombus. Use `?room=dev19&view=computer` for the original and `?room=dev19&view=candidate` for the imported candidate. The desk, chair and drive are the same in both exhibits. The local warehouse workstation is positioned left of the isolator and angled toward the operator. The owner requests dramatic simplification and a visible powered Mode 7 display after first committing/pushing the initial integration.
