# Main disconnect operator

The MAIN DISCONNECT remains a red-handled flange operator on a dark escutcheon, rated for the machine's 100 A feed. Its former rounded, turned grip has been replaced by a flat painted-metal arm with a straight, chamfered insulating sleeve, transverse grip grooves, a flat end cap and visible fasteners. Black-handled operators use the same shared construction.

The visual reference is Allen-Bradley's [1494F-M1 painted-metal operator for 30–200 A](https://www.rockwellautomation.com/en-us/products/details.1494f-m1.html), its [manufacturer photograph](https://www.rockwellautomation.com/resources/images/productinfo/IDW_1000x1000/1494F/1494F-M1_1000x1000.jpg) and [outline drawing 10008084135](https://configurator.rockwellautomation.com/api/Doc/10008084135_00.pdf). The drawing gives a 141.30 mm pivot-to-tip reach and 153-degree throw. These guide the grip and movement; the game's existing escutcheon and control locations are retained rather than claiming a complete reproduction of the catalogue assembly.

The arm swings out ahead of the cabinet, from 13.5 degrees for ON to 166.5 degrees for OFF. Its full sweep must remain clear of the mounting plate, checked in `src/toolkit/components/industrial-control/views/cabinet-fittings.test.ts`. All rooms and galleries reuse this view. The incoming floor isolator uses the separate squared heavy-throw operator on its steel enclosure.
