# Main disconnect operator reference

The dev6 MAIN DISCONNECT bucket is labelled 100 A, re-rated to match the 100 A incoming supply isolator before it. The owner prefers its original red lever on a black escutcheon. Keep that visual design. It uses the shared breaker handle component and has ON above OFF.

The grip is offset forward of the mounting plate. In Three.js, positive X rotation sends its upward arm toward positive Z, out of the cabinet; the handle moves from +0.55 rad at ON to π − 0.55 rad at OFF. The full sweep remains ahead of the plate, checked by `src/toolkit/components/industrial-control/views/cabinet-fittings.test.ts`. Dev0's CABINET FITTINGS board shows the same red lever. The heavy-throw handle (`createHeavyDutyThrowHandle`) was inspired by Allen-Bradley's [1494F-M2](https://www.rockwellautomation.com/en-us/products/details.1494F-M2.html), which Rockwell [lists for 800 and 1200 A breaker frames](https://literature.rockwellautomation.com/idc/groups/literature/documents/td/1494-td002_-en-p.pdf). The incoming supply isolator no longer uses it: the owner chose an enclosed rotary main switch, yellow with a red handle, after Eaton's P3-100/I5/SVB (100 A, three poles, 200 × 280 × 169 mm), with its own rotary handle (`createRotaryIsolatorHandle`).
