## Where things stand

- **The game aims for realism.** The PS1 look is retired: no `?render=ps1`, no P key. `src/rendering/ps1-material.ts` keeps its name but only makes the realistic material for the WebGL rooms.
- **The Hall (dev4) and the plinth room (dev5) draw with WebGPURenderer** (WebGL 2 fallback) through `src/rendering/node-pipeline.ts`: one scene pass into four targets (colour; diffuse with metalness; normal with roughness; velocity), then SSGI (bounced light and AO), SSR, bloom above white and TRAA. `?stage=colour|gi|ao|lit|reflections|reflected` shows one stage. Materials there must be node materials (`onBeforeCompile` is ignored); WebGPU allows only four targets in a pass, so new data must be packed into spare channels. SSR's alpha is ray length, not opacity: its colour is added.
- **Dev0 to dev3 are still WebGL** (`createRenderer` in `src/rendering/render-style.ts`); moving them to WebGPU is open.
- **Dev5, the plinth room** (`src/rendering/plinth-room.ts`, `src/scene/plinth-exhibits.ts`, `src/game/plinths.ts`): a dark gallery with a rhombus, a double helix and a sphere on plinths 20 m apart; within 4 m a plinth's spotlight comes up and the others dim. The tracker reports each arrival with `previous`, `before` and `visit`, dispatched as a `plinth-arrival` event. `devPlinths` has `view`, `active`, `previous` and `arrivals`. Known blemish: a pale screen-space reflection of the lit plinth top on the sphere's underside.
- **The narration script is complete**: 31 clips in `doc/script/plinth-story.md` (the master: file names, moments, lines, the narrator's feeling for each shape), `doc/script/plinth-teleprompter.md` (generated by `python3 doc/script/make-teleprompter.py`, grouped by shape, each clip slated "Clip twelve"), and the whole process in `doc/script/README.md`.

## What happens next

1. **The owner records** the teleprompter script at home as one long file, saying each slate aloud ("Clip twelve", "Clip twelve, again" for a retake; last take wins).
2. **Split it** as `doc/script/README.md` says: transcribe with word timestamps (`mlx_whisper`, or `~/.local/bin/whisper`), cut on the slates and the silences (`ffmpeg`), normalise to about -16 LUFS, export to `src/audio/assets/narration/plinths/<file>.ogg`, report missing or changed clips, credit in `CREDITS.md`.
3. **Wire it in**: add the moments the room does not report yet (leaving a plinth, staying at one, in the dark for a while, near the exit) to `src/game/plinths.ts` with tests; add an exit on dev5's far (east) wall, since clip 29 says the door is "on the other side"; write a narration player that picks the most specific clip, rotates walking-away versions, does not interrupt a playing clip with a lesser one and plays one-off clips once.
