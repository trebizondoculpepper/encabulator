# Module 2: Standard Encabulation Operation — teleprompter

Record all the speeches in one continuous WAV or FLAC. Before each speech, say its clip number aloud, for example, "Clip one." Leave about three seconds of silence, then perform the speech and leave another three seconds before the next slate. The printed words are a guide: improvise within a clip as you like. If you retake it, say "Clip one, again" and perform the whole speech; the last complete take is the one to use. Record each alternative as its own numbered clip. Headings, asset names and italic directions are silent. Do not wait for the game's seven- or twenty-second pauses: those are added during playback. Keep the original recording untrimmed. The editor will identify each slate, cut out the speech between the surrounding gaps, match it to the asset key in the clip index, and replace the temporary AI voice. Any improvisation that changes when a line belongs in the game will be checked with you before it is assigned.

168 clips, including all optional lines and card-name/count alternatives. Alternate versions belong to separate takes, not to one playthrough.
