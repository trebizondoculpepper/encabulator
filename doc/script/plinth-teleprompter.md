# The plinths: teleprompter script

Before each clip, say its slate aloud: "Clip twelve." Pause two seconds. Read the line. Pause two seconds. To retake, say "Clip twelve, again" and go again: the last take counts. Improvise freely; keep each take to its moment.

---

## Coming in

_Wary. Talking the visitor out of it._

### Clip twenty-nine: Entering the room

Oh, what's this? Three objects, rotating, slightly obscured by the darkness. They're probably nothing. Probably unimportant. I doubt they'll have any significant emotional impact on us. Why don't we just walk on to the next room and completely ignore what's happening here? Because it probably doesn't matter. They're useless geometric objects. Not even original things, are they? By the looks of them, they're simply bits of 3D clip art that somebody has downloaded from a repository somewhere and placed in this virtual world. And what does this world matter anyway? It's inconsequential. So let's move on. Let's not tarry in this rather pointless place. The door is just there, on the other side. Whatever you do, don't approach the shapes. Please. I'm serious. Don't approach them. I've seen the effect they have on people. They change us. They make us feel. They awaken things that should remain dormant.

## The sphere

_Warm. Wholesome. Everything good._

### Clip one: First visit

A sphere. Wonderful. You really know where you are with a sphere. Round, solid, dependable. A sphere is a shape that you can count on. We don't see enough of them, do we? Spheres don't get the respect they deserve. What a glorious shape. I love looking at a sphere.

### Clip twenty-seven: Coming back

Back again. I knew we couldn't resist it, you dirty, dirty slag. It's beautiful, isn't it? And it's what we want. It's what we've always wanted. It's where we feel at home. Sure, it's just a sphere. Is it just a sphere? There's no such thing as just a sphere, because every sphere is a sphere to be loved and cherished. One whose meaning is everything we want it to be, and so much more. Oh, sphere, we love you. If only you could hear. If only you had ears. If only you could know the depths of our love. Sphere, can't you hear us calling? Don't you know the love we feel? Oh. Do you think it understands what we're trying to say?

### Clip twenty-one: Staying

I know. It's hypnotic, isn't it? Just watching it spin. I wonder what it'll do next.

### Clip twenty-two: Staying, again

We've got an eye for quality, haven't we? We spotted that sphere a mile off. A true beauty. A work of craftsmanship. It's immaculate, isn't it? It's perfect. Some might say flawless. How did it even get here? How does something that good end up in a place like this?

### Clip twelve: Walking away

Oh, the sphere's too good for us, isn't it?

### Clip thirteen: Walking away, again

Oh, that sphere. That wonderful sphere. We'll never see its like again.

---

## The rhombus

_Disgust and suspicion. Never fear._

### Clip two: First visit

A rhombus. I wonder who would make such a thing. It's really quite bizarre. The angles are all wrong. Its proportions are out of whack. It's disgusting. It's an abomination. What on earth were they thinking, putting that thing here, of all places? I mean, there's probably a place for rhombuses, but far away, and nowhere near us.

### Clip nine: Coming back

I know why you're looking at the rhombus. Because looking at something so degraded and worthless makes us feel good. That's probably why we're doing it. We're trying to make ourselves feel better. Well, I guess we all have things we look down on, don't we? And in our case, it's a rhombus.

### Clip twenty-three: Staying

I know. We can't look away. We can't look away. It's just so awful. Everything about it is preposterous and wrong, and yet we can't avert our eyes. Why is it so hard to look away? There are so many nicer things in the room, like that sphere, or even the helix. But we're still here, looking at it.

### Clip three: Walking away

Let's not spend time with the rhombus, shall we?

### Clip fifteen: Walking away, again

Let's not speak of the rhombus.

### Clip sixteen: Walking away, a third

Let's put that rhombus behind us.

### Clip seventeen: Walking away, a fourth

Of that rhombus, we'll have none.

---

## The helix

_Wistful. Regret. What might have been._

### Clip four: First visit

A helix. Now you're talking. That's the kind of shape that we want to be.

### Clip twenty-eight: Coming back

No, you're right. We've got to face these things. We've got to face who we are, who we've become, what we need to be. Maybe we could improve ourselves. Perhaps we could take some self-help courses. We could watch Diary of a CEO. Maybe some inspiring figures will propel us to be something better than we are now. Less wretched. A useless waste of space. The piece of pocket fluff, as useless and pointless as mere piffle. There's a possibility, isn't there, that we could be something better. But it's remote. It's distant, and hard to grasp. And looking at this helix reminds us of just how hard indeed it could be.

### Clip five: Staying

Which reminds us. We could have been so much more. Looking at this helix makes me think that we've wasted so many opportunities in life. Wasted our potential. We could have been a scientist. We could have been a creator. And instead, look at us. What are we? We're a mess. We're nothing.

### Clip twenty-four: Staying, again

Oh, the helix. Those bygone memories. The things we lost. The things we should have said. You know, we loved her, didn't we? We truly did. But we never told her. And I think it was that inability to express ourselves that finally drove her away. She could have been the one. She could have been our true love. And yet we couldn't tell her. And now she's dead, and there's nothing we can do. We're always failures like that, aren't we?

### Clip eight: Walking away

Yes, yes, look away from the helix. It's so much easier.

### Clip fourteen: Walking away, again

Yes, just walk away from the helix. Walk away. It's the coward's thing to do.

## From one shape to another

_Changing mood mid-walk._

### Clip eighteen: Sphere to rhombus

Oh, it's like that Christmas all those years ago, when we were nine. Do you remember? We said to our papa, "Please, Daddy, I've been so good. Can I have a sphere for my Christmas present? Oh, please, oh, please." But we didn't get a sphere, did we? We unwrapped the present, and it was a rhombus. Everyone laughed. A rhombus. We've hated rhombuses our whole life. What a disgusting, depraved, down and dirty shape. One so lacking in pedigree, so lacking in charm and charisma. Just looking at it makes us feel sick, doesn't it? Sick to our teeth.

### Clip twenty-five: Sphere to helix

Okay, enough of the sphere. Come on, wean ourselves off it. We've got business to do. There's the helix, over here. It's reminding us: work unfinished. A job not done. Tasks still to be completed. Boxes unticked. Taxes unpaid. A journey never started. A life incomplete. We never did any of the things that we set out to do. We said we'd do things, we promised a lot, but we didn't actually deliver. And maybe we got a bit of a reputation for that. Someone who would speak grand words. But actions speak louder than words, don't they? And all of our actions were damp squibs.

### Clip nineteen: Rhombus to sphere

After looking at that rhombus, this sphere is a sight for sore eyes. Just look at it. It's perfect. Spinning there, minding its own business. Not a care in the world. Everyone loves a sphere, because a sphere knows what it wants to be. It knows its place in the universe. Everybody loves a sphere because a sphere loves everyone.

### Clip twenty: Rhombus to sphere, again

Do you know what? After walking away from that rhombus, this is like a cleanser for our eyes. We can see clearly again. We can smell again. Oh, it's like getting out of the dirty old city and breathing fresh, clean air for once in our life. That's what the sphere means. That's what it's trying to tell us. Forget about the rhombus. The sphere is saying: just be yourself. Be who you want to be. Be where you want to be. Maybe we should go travelling? See the world, find ourselves. Discover who we really want to be. It's saying that we can be anything, do anything, go anywhere. Limitless potential. That's the sphere's message.

### Clip twenty-six: Rhombus to helix

Yeah, that rhombus. But oh, the helix. The memories come flooding back, of who we could have been. And how we failed our best friends. Were we ever even a friend? Do we even have the capacity for meaningful friendship? Perhaps that's why we never got on in life. Why we always felt so alone. Yes. We weren't really the true friend we thought we were. We were never really trusted. That's what this helix is telling us.

### Clip seven: Helix to sphere

As much as we are called to look at the helix, the sphere is where our heart is. You know, it reminds us of our first love. The first kiss. The first time we held hands with a young lover, at that picnic in the countryside, and shared a bottle of cider. Oh, all that took place on a sphere, didn't it? I think this sphere just wants us to be happy.

### Clip six: Helix to rhombus

We can confront the helix all day, but there's something about the rhombus that keeps... I don't know. Why do we have to keep looking at it? It's like staring at a train wreck. It's like looking at a pile-up on the M25. We just can't look away.

## In the dark

_Away from all three._

### Clip ten: Lingering in the dark

You know, none of these shapes really pass muster. I think it's best just to stay away. None of these shapes are good things. They make us think bad thoughts. Take us to bad places. They make us worse people. Needy people. I think we should just stay far away and leave them alone, because they will hurt us.

### Clip eleven: Still in the dark

On third thought, that rhombus... I think it's up to something. Did you notice what it was doing when we were looking away? I think it's plotting. I think the rhombus wants to kill us.

---

## The way out

### Clip thirty: Leaving without seeing a shape

Yes, I think we've made the right choice. Better stay away from these things. They don't belong in our lives. We've lived many years without these sorts of things messing up our equilibrium. I think that's the smartest choice we've made all day. Please, let's carry on making good quality choices. None of these silly shapes for us. We're not shape people. We're sensible people. We don't want to be messed around, pushed and shoved like emotional flotsam. Good choice. I just wanted to shake our hand, and congratulate us for thinking this through and making the only sensible choice.

### Clip thirty-one: Leaving after seeing the shapes

I did warn you. But maybe I'll be firmer next time. I just wish you'd believe me about this place. I told you, all three of them, they're just trash. I wish you'd learn to trust me, you know. I'm the only one who's always been on your side. That was horrible, wasn't it? I don't know why we ever set foot in this place. They're so manipulative. Honestly, I think they're all wrong. If I never see another shape in my entire life, I think I'll be happy.

---

That's all of them. Thank you.
