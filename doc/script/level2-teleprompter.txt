MODULE 2: STANDARD ENCABULATION OPERATION
TELEPROMPTER / RECORDING SCRIPT

Record one WAV for the whole session. Before each clip, say its number aloud, for example, "Clip one." Pause about two seconds, read the speech, then pause another two seconds. For a retake, say "Clip one, again" and repeat the whole clip. Read only the speech and the spoken clip number; headings, asset names and italic directions are silent. Do not wait for the game's seven- or twenty-second pauses: those are added during playback. Keep the original WAV untrimmed so the slates and gaps can guide editing.

173 clips. Directions and asset IDs are not spoken.
