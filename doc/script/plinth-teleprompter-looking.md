# The plinths: looking lines

Short lines for standing at a shape and looking at it. No slates: read each line, then pause about three seconds before the next. To retake a line, just say it again; the last take counts. Improvise freely.

---

## The sphere

_Warm. Wholesome. Everything good._

What a wonderful sphere. We could look at it for hours.

It's so unpredictable, isn't it?

Looking at it just makes us feel happy.

I don't think we've ever known joy like looking at this sphere.

Let's stay looking at this sphere forever. We can be happy here.

---

## The helix

_Wistful. Regret. What might have been._

It's so painful, staring into the abyss of this helix.

This helix is reminding us of everything we've ever done wrong in our lives.

Is there a more painful way to confront our failures than looking at this helix?

Looking at this helix makes our head hurt.

---

## The rhombus

_Disgust and suspicion. Never fear._

This rhombus is just so wrong. Like some Lovecraftian nightmare.

A fearful geometry. It's unholy.

We should just put it out of its misery.

This is a truly horrible thing. Do not suffer the rhombus to live.

What on earth is this place coming to? Allowing rhombuses in here?

---

That's all of them. Thank you.
