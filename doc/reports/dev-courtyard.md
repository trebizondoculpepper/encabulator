# Report: dev_courtyard, the courtyard and its skies

Prompt: `doc/prompts/011-dev-courtyard-sky.md`.

## 2026-10-19: the courtyard built

Built: the room (`src/rendering/dev-courtyard-room.ts`, `RoomId` `dev_courtyard`, in `ROOMS` but not the portal chain, and not a door of its own on the corridor), the scene (`src/scene/dev-courtyard.ts`: an 18 m square light well three storeys deep, open to the sky, its four facades with windows, parapet and pipes, a spring door in the middle of each wall back to its side of dev_corridor, paving, planters, ashtrays, benches, chairs, a bin, a SMOKING AREA sign), the furniture placeholders (`src/toolkit/components/site-dressing/views/courtyard-props.ts`), the skies (`src/game/skies.ts`, tested: presets, blending, a changer taking six seconds, the sun's direction), the sky dome (`src/scene/sky-dome.ts`: one shader pass with a gradient, sun, up to three cloud layers, stars) and a control pedestal with the owner's three ambient settings: OVERCAST (the default), FLUFFY CLOUDS and HELL (a parody of Quake's skies: three layers of purple noise racing over a dark purple sky). The extra skies (clear, cirrus, storm, sunset, night) are reachable by `devCourtyard.sky(id)`.

Checked: the five checks (805 tests, including a courtyard budget test); in the browser each of the three skies from the start view and looking up, and the pedestal; about 430 draw calls including the sun's shadow pass. Sky colours are given as sRGB; reading them as linear washed the first HELL out to pale pink.

Limitations and open questions: the doors lead to dark lobbies until prompt 009; the courtyard's light under HELL is lit a little warmly by the room's environment map; no rain, wind or sound with the skies; the corridor has no windows onto the courtyard (later, with the see-through logic).

Later the same day: the ground-floor windows were taken out (the corridor behind them has none), and HELL was redrawn as Quake draws its skies, after the owner asked for holes in the noise so the layers can be seen: Quake's sky texture is two halves, a solid backdrop and an overlay whose black pixels are transparent, scrolled diagonally, the overlay twice as fast (GLQuake: time × 8 and × 16; InsideQC, https://forums.insideqc.com/viewtopic.php?p=24089 and https://forums.insideqc.com/viewtopic.php?p=33599; GameDev.net, https://gamedev.net/blogs/entry/1531387/). The sky dome now has a `quake` mode: a solid noise back layer and up to two cloud layers over it with holes, each twice as fast. Its colours are taken from the mauve ramp of Quake's palette (palette.lmp from https://opengameart.org/content/quake-i-palette-and-colormaps, indices 144 to 159: #bb739f down to #2f171b).

Then the owner asked for lights and a night sky: a light switch by every door (one circuit, intermediate switching, so any switch flips them all), a round cool white LED bulkhead on each wall beside its door (`createBulkheadLight`, a placeholder) and a lamp post with a Victorian-style lantern (`createLampPost`, a placeholder), five real lights in all; and NIGHT on the pedestal beside OVERCAST, FLUFFY CLOUDS and HELL. `devCourtyard.lights(on)` switches them in development. Checked at night with the lights on and off.
