# Picking up and dropping Pepperdyne boards

Added portable component state and a shared first-person pickup/drop handler for dev0 and dev6. The existing Use action picks up a card, backplane assembly or capacitor board, disconnecting it from its circuit. The same action drops the held object wherever the player is looking. The exact camera-relative pickup point and distance are preserved while walking and looking, with original world scale maintained when objects leave scaled cabinet/plinth groups. Mouse/E, touch tap and gamepad A all go through the one control implementation; there are no new bindings or room-specific inputs.

Dropped boards fall, turn onto their side/face, bounce lightly, skitter and settle quickly. A small fixed-step pure motion model handles gravity, damping and contacts with the floor, plinth tops and MCC bucket shelves. Each board owns its synthesised positional clatter and two short dust puffs. Dropped boards remain pickable. Electrical repair controls cannot reconnect a physically removed card. The backplanes and removable cards now carry the fictional Pepperdyne maker marking; real catalogue products remain physical reference models rather than an in-world manufacturer claim.

Verified pickup, sideways movement, drop and impact through the shared controls in Chrome. Added tests for pickup/drop state, stable settling at multiple frame rates, fixed camera distance through walking and turning, scale preservation, floor contact, occlusion, repeated pickup, and electrical isolation after removal. The planned training chapter ending (smoking machine, narrator reveal and waste-bin disposal sequence) is context for this prototype, not yet a scripted segment.

Typecheck, lint, formatting, all 296 tests and the production build passed. Changes remain uncommitted.
