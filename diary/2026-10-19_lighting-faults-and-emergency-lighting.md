# Lighting faults and emergency lighting (dev18)

The owner wants the machine in Level 0 to affect the warehouse's lights (flicker, a brownout when it is run hard for long, the lights going out entirely in its catastrophic failures) and emergency lighting that comes on when the lighting fails, with a darker day so the room's own lights matter. They asked for every part and system to be modelled first in the building fittings gallery (dev18), with main lighting, a switch, a way to test-trip the lighting circuit, and a way to check that the fire exits are well lit; and, once the work was under way, that the parts be modelled on real products, and then that they be simple placeholders, the effort going into the game's behaviour.

Two research passes found the products and the facts: `doc/research/emergency-lighting-parts.md` (Thorn Voyager Star emergency downlights and Thorn's spacing guide for 7 m, a LEDVANCE emergency bulkhead, an exit box sign, MK's EMG LTG TEST key switch, Hager MCBs and RCBOs, the Aquaforce Pro batten; EN 60598-2-22 changeover, LED driver behaviour in sags, BS 5266-1's figures) and `doc/research/warehouse-lighting-control.md` (a warehouse this size is switched by zone from a metalclad grid switch bank by the door, with high-bay presence detectors per zone, not one domestic switch, as the owner suspected).

Built, all pure logic tested: the supply's quality (`supply-quality.ts`: normal, brownout, severe), an LED driver per luminaire (`led-driver.ts`: steady, then dimming and flickering, then dropping out and restarting, each a little different), a per-fitting emergency changeover (`emergency-changeover.ts`: each at its own threshold between 62 and 78 % of rated, so a sag lights some), and the BS 5266-1 check (`emergency-lighting.ts`: open-area lux, uniformity, a light by every way out). The building services model gained the supply, lighting zones, a lighting trip (and a severe disturbance's trip), and the voltage at the luminaires and the emergency fittings; its test key now cuts only the emergency fittings' supply, as a real one does, where before it also put the room's lights out. In dev18 the room is now lit only by its own battens, switched by zone from a grid switch bank with the test key, with emergency downlights, door bulkheads and a maintained exit sign, a SUPPLY selector and a LIGHTING FAULT button by the board, and a lux map and `check()` for the exits.

What we learned: the emergency light's floor level and what the renderer shows are different things, so the BS 5266 check is done on the plan with real lumens, and the render is tuned to look like emergency lighting rather than to be photometric. Running frames by hand (`devFittingsGallery.shot`) gets pictures out of a background tab, where the browser does not draw. Next: Level 0 (a darker day, its warehouse switched by zone, its machine's load driving the supply, catastrophic failures surging and tripping the lights), the presence detectors, and better models by another agent.

Later the same day, on the owner's go-ahead, the briefing was written as numbered prompts (`doc/prompts/001` to `006`; numbering prompts is now a rule in `AGENTS.md`), Codex was sent off in the background to improve the fittings' models (prompt 006, `doc/reports/lighting-fitting-models.md`), and prompts 002 to 005 were carried out: switch-start fluorescent battens whose tubes come to life one by one (tuned to fresh research, `doc/research/fluorescent-battens.md`), Level 0's darker day with the room's light following the tubes (the environment light had been lighting the room regardless, which is why switching the lights off first did little), zoned lighting and BS 5266 emergency lighting in the warehouse, the machine's load driving the building's supply (motor starts, over-capacity brownouts), catastrophes surging and tripping the lights, strangeness dimming tubes, and two new simulator switches. The details and the open questions are in `doc/reports/level0-lighting.md`. Learned: on switch-start gear a brownout is far darker than on LEDs, because a tube that drops out cannot restrike until the supply is back above about 85 %; and a hidden browser tab throttles the game, so long timings are better proved by tests than by watching.
