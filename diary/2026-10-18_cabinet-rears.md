# Cabinet rear implementation and workstation follow-up

Implemented the researched seven-section rear design in the shared MCC assembly, reaching dev6, Level0 and Level2. Replaced the outer continuous sheet with individual fixed panels and three closed-by-default rear service doors in the control and automation sections. Added guarded horizontal/vertical busways, covered 60×40 mm duct, mounting-plate rear connectors and supported tails, blanked entry plates, paired drive filters and moving green/yellow protective bonds. Preserved existing opaque inner mounting plates, component identities, front-door indices and electrical state. Reusable rear fittings are exhibited at true size on dev0's new east-wall board.

Rear access participates in bucket culling independently of front access, including closing animations. The PLC window's always-render setting remains. Dev6's room depth grows from 10 to 12 m so rear door swings and walking have clearance; warehouse machine placements are unchanged. Added rear inspection starts and development-only render statistics.

The BBC cable follow-up measures the imported model's actual rear connector positions separately from the procedural baseline. Mains cords now terminate at those connectors and distinct floor plugs and remain outside the desktop; video joins the Cub and BBC RGB sockets. The cabinet data connection remains deferred. Fixed keyboard replacement to avoid collecting rear switches and sockets that happened to share the spacebar material. Startup now displays `>CHAIN "DIAG"`, following the BBC Micro User Guide's BASIC loading syntax (https://www.bbcmicrobot.com/docs/BBC_User_Guide.pdf).

The shared first-person controller now restores normal height on C/V, touch crouch or gamepad crouch when already at a non-standard height. Explicit placement clears stale crouch state, preventing V from combining chair height with a second crouch offset and taking the eye below the floor. Meaningful tests cover seated/raised resets, both BBC cable geometries, independent rear/front access, culling through motion, mounting-plate occlusion and unchanged machine state.
