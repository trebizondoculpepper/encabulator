# Level 0 rendering performance

The owner worried that Level 0 had grown too complicated to draw at 30 frames a second on a cheap machine, and asked for rules to keep it there. Measured in the browser, the room was limited by draw calls and object count, not triangles: about 3,700 draw calls and 24,000 objects a frame, 17 ms of CPU a frame on an M3 Max. The line-up alone was 18,000 meshes, 11,000 of them lamps that were made and hidden for good.

Three rules were adopted (AGENTS.md, Performance): a draw budget, tested without a GPU by `countDraws` from each room's standard views; merging everything that does not change once it is built (`mergeStatic`, `mergeTree`), with what moves, hides or relights marked `userData.live` where it is built; and culling merged detail too small to see (`createDetailCuller`). Clickable parts are taken to move unless their part marks them `still`, in which case they are merged with their door and their own meshes kept, hidden, as click targets (a raycaster does not care whether a mesh is shown).

The hard part was knowing what moves. A watcher (`watchMerged`) records every merged-away mesh and what it hung from, and the merge tests work everything (every input both ways, doors, failures, boards, the warehouse's switches) and check after every step that none of it changed. Checking only at the end missed buttons that go in and spring back; checking every step found about 25 moving pieces (needles, rockers, lamps swapped by material, terminal screws, extinguisher pins). Culling was tuned by comparing renders against `?merge=off`: at the Hall's threshold the start view lost its meters, gauges and bezels, so the threshold was made finer, separate for controls, zoom-aware, and judged per piece rather than per merged mesh.

Result, after rebasing onto the scenario simulator, rear enclosure and fire system that landed meanwhile (the simulator and its rack merged too, the side culling now finding doors by their named hinges): from the start view 1,375 draw calls in the browser (3,771 with `?merge=off`), 8.7 ms of CPU a frame on the M3 Max (18.3 ms); from the far corner 1,420 (5,039), 9.2 ms (25.7 ms); renders matching `?merge=off`. Level 0 is allowed 1,400 for now. What is left are the printed legends (a canvas texture each, so they cannot merge) and the lamps (a paint each, so they can light alone): a label atlas and per-vertex lamp colours would be the next steps toward 1,000.
