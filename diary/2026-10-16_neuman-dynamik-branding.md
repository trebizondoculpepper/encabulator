# Neuman Dynamik cabinet branding

Established Neuman Dynamik as the Encabulator's German electronics manufacturer. Each of the seven MCC cabinet sections now carries a physical, screw-fixed NEUMAN DYNAMIK nameplate on its fixed upper trim, with warm white lettering on charcoal and a restrained red keyline. The plaques reuse the engraved nameplate view and remain clear of doors, controls and the plant message display. The former Allen-Bradley lineup badge is replaced; supplier markings on internal components, especially the rival Pepperdyne cards, remain appropriate to those parts.

The change is presentation only and adds no controls or input handling. Doors retain their closed default state. Changes remain uncommitted.

Validation: typecheck, lint, formatting check, all 296 tests and production build pass. The build retains its existing large-chunk advisory.
