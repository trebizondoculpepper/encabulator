# Retro Computing workstation

Ported the original VX3 BBC Micro Model B, Cub, floppy drive, desk and office chair into reusable toolkit views/parts and Dev19, Retro Computing. The baseline starts off and has a startup cue, loading phase and standalone diagnostic display. Added gallery portals, universal-control interaction and tests for startup, power cycling and sound cancellation. Credited all reused models and sound provenance in CREDITS.md.

Researched existing replacement meshes and key-switch head designs. Downloaded Ant Mercer's publicly served VirtualBeeb GLB for local comparison beside the baseline, with attribution and source terms recorded. It remains development-only pending separate production rights confirmation. A paid alternative was found but not purchased. Added the workstation beside the incoming isolator in Level 0 and Level 2, forward and angled toward the operating position. Restored the requested darker, switch-controlled Level 0 lighting while integrating current sandbox wiring.

Changed throw-switch, disconnect and internal breaker sounds to lower, heavier procedural thocks. Key geometry remains unchanged; findings and the Schneider photograph are saved for the next design pass. The owner requested a commit/push of the current state before simplifying the imported mesh and fixing its Cub display.
