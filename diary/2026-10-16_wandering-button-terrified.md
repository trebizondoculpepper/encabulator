# Larger mode panel and Terrified corner escapes

Replaced dev9's small two-hole station with a 300 × 300 × 200 mm steel panel after Schneider's PanelSeT CRN NSYCRN33200 (https://www.se.com/sg/en/product/NSYCRN33200/). It has one four-position rotary MODE selector, labelled NORMAL, EAGER, SKITTISH and TERRIFIED, with four labelled XB4 pilot lights below it showing the active mode. Kept the separate PRESSED lamp. The selector is a reusable cam-selector view after Harmony K1D004NLH (https://www.se.com/nl/en/product/K1D004NLH/): a 45 mm square escutcheon, 35 mm handle and four maintained 45-degree detents. Added the same four-position part to dev0's CONTROLS board before using it here. All devices retain true size and the shared controls scheme.

After discussion, the owner agreed that TERRIFIED first goes to the corner furthest from the player, then makes three subsequent approach-triggered escapes to randomly selected other corners. The initial trip does not count. The motion model routes around the player while travelling, keeps the stand in bounds and prevents repeated counts while still travelling to the selected corner. Upon reaching the third escape corner it gives up, stays still and turns its button towards the player. Pressing it in TERRIFIED produces a brief spray of sparks from the lens and immediately resets the mode, selector and active pilot to NORMAL. Spark timing belongs to the world model and drawing reuses the existing ember spray, respects reduced motion and takes no clicks. No new sound assets were introduced.

Regression tests cover the initial destination, three complete relocations and surrender, random corner choice, route clearance and bounds, re-entering the mode, the spark lifetime and the immediate mode reset. All 260 tests, typecheck, lint, format check and production build passed. Inspected the enlarged panel and selector/pilot changes in Chrome; no browser errors were reported. Updated the dev9 agent-guide entry. Left the owner's existing main.py changes untouched.

The owner subsequently clarified that panel indicators must not be pressable. Added a reusable `pushToTest: false` pilot-light option and applied it to the four mode lamps and the PRESSED indicator. These lamps register no use action or press sound; their state comes solely from the model. Existing push-to-test lamps elsewhere retain their behaviour.

The owner refined the orientations: TERRIFIED now turns towards the geometric centre of the room during the final two metres of every corner journey and keeps facing inward while waiting, including after surrender. EAGER turns its red lens square-on to the horizontal viewing axis immediately, then travels once aligned and the four-second wait has elapsed. Translation uses its own delayed time step, so waiting no longer freezes rotation. The stillness check now tolerates tiny camera jitter and compares accumulated changes against the last meaningful pose. Added regression coverage for all corner orientations, immediate eager alignment, the complete delayed approach and facing on arrival, and small camera jitter.

Final EAGER refinement: wait three seconds after meaningful camera motion stops, ignoring the existing small jitter tolerance, then finish turning before travelling. The delay now covers rotation as well as translation. Tightened the alignment threshold and tested the final physical lens position against the sight line to six decimal places, along with restarting the delay on camera movement.

The owner superseded the sequential EAGER motion: turning and translation now start together after a random 1–4 second still-camera wait. The delay is sampled once when entering EAGER or after meaningful camera motion; tiny jitter does not resample it. Each journey uses independent integrated smoothstep velocity ramps for translation (0.6 s ramps, 0.5 m/s cap) and shortest-path rotation (0.5 s ramps, 1.5 rad/s cap), with constant-speed cruise where distance allows, lower peak speeds for short adjustments, and exact endpoint landing. Tests verify simultaneous starts, random-delay endpoints and resampling, acceleration/braking and speed limits, short moves without overshoot, and final lens alignment.

EAGER now uses the actual lens-height intersection only when reachable within its distance and wall constraints. Looking level, up, too steeply down, beyond its range or through a wall falls back to a position 0.8 m in front of the player. If that space is blocked by a wall, it chooses the nearest clear spot beside it without overlapping the player. Final orientation faces the player from the chosen destination. Added regression coverage for these unreachable targets, near-wall clearance and a complete smoothed fallback journey; the random delay and motion ramps are retained.

Final room polish: unreachable EAGER targets now bring the lens to 0.5 m from the player, with continuously player-facing, speed-limited steering during the approach. The red lens is now an illuminated momentary part with an independent world-model heartbeat: NORMAL off; EAGER 60 bpm, SKITTISH 90 bpm and TERRIFIED 120 bpm, each double-pulsed. Presses retain their separate PRESSED lamp. Pressing TERRIFIED sparks, selects NORMAL and leaves the stand dormant in place until another mode is selected. The reusable momentary part and dev0 board now support a separate lamp drive.
