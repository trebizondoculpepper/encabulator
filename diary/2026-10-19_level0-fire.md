# Level 0's fire, end to end

The owner asked, going to bed, to be able to start a fire and watch the whole chain: the fire, the alarm, the sprinklers, the electrical fault and a ruined machine. It now runs from the scenario simulator's FIRE switch under FAULTS. Three lower cabinets burn, smoking and sooting the faces above them; the smoke sets the warehouse's roof detectors off and the voice alarm starts its investigation; the fire, fed by the live bus, grows until its heat bursts the sprinkler heads over the line-up, whatever the panel is told; their water floods the floor, shorts the live bus with flashovers, trips everything out and leaves the machine ruined. Cutting the power early lets the fire die down before the heads go, the realistic save the owner chose. Before that, the warehouse's fire fittings, each an unconnected specimen until now (the owner pressed a call point and nothing happened), were wired to Level 0's fire alarm system, extracted from dev8's model so both rooms share it, and SPRINKLER joined the faults and symptoms. The CO₂ extinguisher waits on being carryable.
