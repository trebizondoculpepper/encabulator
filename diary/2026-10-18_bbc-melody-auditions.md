# BBC melody auditions

Downloaded the owner’s YouTube reference, Fizgog’s “BBC Micro — Sound Chip”, and prepared four labelled pairs of cleaned recordings and approximate chip-style reconstructions. Retained local raw audio/metadata, recorded provenance in CREDITS.md, and kept the candidates outside production assets. The first phrase has a severe level change and an obscured opening, so the reconstruction is explicitly an approximation rather than claimed note-perfect. The working library includes lossless WAVs, MP3 previews, editable note scores, a reproducible Python processing script, a comparison reel and an audition page. Proposed readiness gating is documented alongside the candidates: authoritative steady/nominal state, BBC on and linked, one cue per stable transition, cancellation and re-arming. Awaiting sound selection before integrating a game cue.
