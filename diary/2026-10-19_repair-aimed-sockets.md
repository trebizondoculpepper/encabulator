# Repair socket aiming

The owner found dev20's carried-object proximity placement unreliable and asked for receptacle targeting. Documented the change in `doc/plans/repair-aimed-sockets.md` and implemented transparent, narrow hit areas over each PLC card slot and the fuse holder. While carrying, the crosshair becomes a green circle over a compatible isolated empty socket or a red X over a rejecting one, including when mouse pointer lock is unavailable. Use tests the same target and decision, then aligns and moves the object into the socket before connecting it. Other aim points retain ordinary drop and magnetic skip disposal. Removed the 20 cm connector-distance requirement from the decision model. Browser verification in dev20 showed the green circle on an empty slot, successful seating with INSTALLED feedback, and a red X plus SLOT OCCUPIED refusal on an occupied slot.
