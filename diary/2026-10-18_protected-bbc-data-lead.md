# Protected BBC data cable

The owner authorized the physical machine data lead after the cabinet backs were finished: a cable from the BBC rear to the floor, under a rubber floor protector, and into one of the low round ports on section 1. Added an RS423 socket anchor for both the procedural and imported computer, a DIN plug and continuous cable down behind the desk, a supported floor route behind the incoming isolator, and a short rise into the existing right-hand round entry. The blanking cap is replaced by a labelled DATA gland; a shared scene anchor defines the actual endpoint instead of duplicating its coordinates in each room. Level0 and Level2 use the same arrangement. Diagnostic program/handshake behaviour remains a separate planning prompt.

The reusable floor protector follows the D-Line FC83 profile, 83 × 14 mm with a real 30 × 10 mm underside channel. Its sweep stays upright around floor bends and has a matte rubber appearance. At the owner's request it sits exactly 1 mm above the floor to avoid coplanar flicker. It is also a registered architectural fitting, exhibited in dev18 and the warehouse gallery. Credits record the manufacturer's profile reference; geometry and materials are original project work.

Tests cover cover dimensions, upright normals, hollow channel, 1 mm clearance, true entry-port transforms at non-unit scene scale, continuous joins between computer drop/floor run/cabinet rise, and clearance behind the isolator. The Level0 `?view=data-cable` inspection start shows the complete route. Browser inspection confirmed the continuous leads and stable cover surface.
