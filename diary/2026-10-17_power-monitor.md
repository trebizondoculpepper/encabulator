# Power monitor display

Repositioned the digital power monitor's three LED rows and lower keys so its small POWERMONITOR title clears the voltage row. Set the dev6 Main Disconnect preview current to a bounded 47 ± 2 A variation and calculate displayed kW from that current, the displayed voltage and an assumed 0.86 power factor. These are still demonstration values; they do not yet respond to individual machine loads.

Audited attached component sounds for the owner. Dev6 has local switch and mechanism sounds throughout the buckets, plus continuous motor, fan, drive fan, pump and blower loops on selected sections. Those section loops currently follow the main feed rather than individual subsystem run state.
