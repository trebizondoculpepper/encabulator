# Skip dressing probabilities

Set each skip's graffiti chance to 60% and lamp chance to 50%, independently. A tagged skip now displays one randomly selected tag from the dev16 graffiti collection on a long side beside its hire lettering. Dressing is applied when the skip appears in Level 2, dev15, and dev14, including each separately spawned dev15 clone. Graffiti geometry is removed with its skip; materials are shared per room kit.
