# Main disconnect handle

The first attempt replaced the clipping Main Disconnect lever with a 194R rotary operator. The owner rejected its proportions and turning action. A second attempt used a grey and black heavy throw handle, but the owner preferred the original red lever on the black plate. Restored that lever in dev6 and dev0. The first clearance fix got Three.js's X-axis rotation sign backwards and pushed the lever into the cabinet. Corrected the arc to positive X rotation, which keeps both end positions and the full sweep in front of the plate, and added a geometry clearance test. The separate incoming supply isolator retains its own heavy throw handle. See `doc/research/main-disconnect-operator.md`.

Refined the original red lever with a rounded, tapered grip and a dark collar at its base. The Main Disconnect breaker now plays its ON/OFF click at a slightly lower level than the other bucket breakers. The shared handle and breaker still operate together.
