## Problem Statement

The owner liked the mini skip from dev14 and wanted it reusable, and used for a gag in Level 2: there is no bin at the start. When the player removes the first Pepperdyne card and is told to get rid of it, they will naturally drop it on the floor; the narrator then says "Were you born in a barn? Put it away in the proper receptacle. There's a skip over there.", and the skip appears behind the player, out of view, as if it had been there all along. Then: on the first drop the camera should glance down at the card, the skip should take one of three fixed places where the player is neither standing nor looking, and it should be about ten seconds' walk from the machine, to leave time for narration.

## Our Approach

Split what dev14 had into reusable pieces: the skip model (`src/scene/skip.ts`) and the throwing behaviour (`src/rendering/bin-tossing.ts`, moved out of `bin-room.ts`): a room adds bins at any time and passes `divert` to `createFirstPerson`, and gets an `onLanded` callback. The choice of place is pure logic, `hiddenSpot` in `src/game/hidden-spot.ts` (tested): every corner of the thing's footprint further off the line of sight than half the horizontal field of view plus 20°, not too near the eye, preferring straight behind.

## Implementation

Level 2 lost its grey box bin and its hand-rolled "card within the bin's square" test. The room is now 24 m long (walking is 2 m/s, so twenty metres is ten seconds), with a fourth row of ceiling lamps every six metres; `SKIP_SPOTS` are three places at the far end, about 20 m from the machine. The first card to settle on the floor while there is no skip starts a glance (the room holds the player and eases the view onto the card in 0.7 s, holds 1.1 s, and lets go), conjures the skip at a hidden spot clear of any loose card, and plays the new line, rendered with the Kokoro placeholder voice as `skip_appears` in both manifests. Later floor drops play the old 104/105 as before. Cards landing in the skip call `onBinned` (they now stay visible, piling up, instead of vanishing), and the per-frame unlocking skips binned cards so they stay put. The script (`doc/scenarios/2-normal_operaion.md`) has a new section, "The skip that was always there", and calls the bin a skip throughout.

## Outcomes

Typecheck, lint, format, tests and build pass. In Chrome, `level2.conjureSkip()` from the machine put the skip at the far end, out of sight, and turning round showed it there. The full card sequence has still not been played end to end.
