# Level0 spares workbench

Adapted the owner's VX3 hardwood-and-steel workbench and blue vice as a freestanding toolkit furnishing, credited in CREDITS.md. Its long edge is perpendicular to the Encabulator. Applied the subsequent placement changes: 2 m south and 1 m east, then 2 m west. Kept its collision footprint aligned with those placements. The dark wooden front trim now sits outside the worktop and 1 mm below its top, removing coplanar overlap on the bench's west edge.

Initially stocked the catalogue, then corrected this at the owner’s request to stock only actual removable machine components plus control-fuse cartridges: the five PLC card types, the VFD capacitor board and a control fuse (seven types). Whole cabinets, rack chassis, motors, fixed motherboards and unrelated gallery examples are not stocked. Automatic packing uses model geometry rather than invisible particle sprites, keeps each stock position clear of the vice and its neighbors, and puts models on the top surface. Assemblies are carried as a whole. PLC spares use the machine's existing spare-card factory and can occupy their compatible backplane slots. The rest retain their own component behavior and failure state; stocking them does not by itself introduce generic replacement sockets for every cabinet device.

Each stock position starts a separate 60-second replenishment timer on its item's pickup, not on installation or disposal. Further handling of the old item cannot restart the timer or consume its replacement. Fresh stock materializes with the existing effect. Added exact timer-boundary and independent-stock tests and a complete-inventory scene test. `?room=level0&view=spares` opens an inspection view.

Level0's registered skip uses the shared placement target and nearby-bin feedback: green when the held object can be tossed, red when aimed at a destination outside range, with the same acceptance check used by placement. This also applies to bins registered through the shared tossing component. Existing tests cover valid placement, out-of-range rejection and nearby magnetic release.
