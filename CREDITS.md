# Audio credits

This is the central credit record for the project's sound effects. New recordings list the author (with a profile or website where available), the original source page, the licence stated there, the local filenames and any edits. The recordings below were carried over from the VX3 Simulator (`../vx3_simulator`), with the edits made there.

## Procedural effects

Control clicks, hums and other synthesised sounds are original procedural patches in `src/audio/`. They use Web Audio and [ZzFX](https://github.com/KilledByAPixel/ZzFX), by [Frank Force](https://github.com/KilledByAPixel), MIT.
