# Audio asset record

The 31 numbered plinth-room narrations in `narration/plinths/clip_01.mp3` through `clip_31.mp3`, plus two intro continuation files `clip_32.mp3` and `clip_33.mp3`, come from the owner-supplied cuts in `resources/sound/narrations/rhombus_room/`. They are mono 44.1 kHz MP3 at 96 kb/s for broad browser playback, high-passed at 80 Hz and normalised with FFmpeg loudnorm targeting -16 LUFS, -1.5 dB true peak and 11 LU loudness range. Short fades were applied to the working WAV cuts. The clip numbers and original source times are in that folder's `clips.csv`; 26 and 31 were identified by their script lines because no spoken slate was audible at those boundaries.

The additional owner recording `TX02_MIC009_20261018_035303_orig.wav` was copied unchanged into `tmp/audio/` from a read-only volume. Fourteen scripted looking lines and two improvised sphere remarks were cut into `resources/sound/narrations/rhombus_room/additional/` and delivered as game clips 34–49. The source times and transcripts are in `additional/clips.csv`. Each working cut is mono 48 kHz PCM float with short edge fades; the game MP3s use the same dialogue processing as the first recording. They join the lingering-line rotation, with at most two linger cues per plinth visit.

The room intro (script clip 29) is delivered as three passages: 29 (noticing the shapes and suggesting we ignore them), 32 (dismissing them as clip art), and 33 (pointing to the exit and warning us away). The third passage splices in the clean second take of “They change us. They make us feel. They awaken things that should remain dormant” from 88.02–96.08 s of the local master recording. The Nutmeg interruption and first take are omitted. Playback leaves 4.5 seconds between passages and yields to a plinth arrival after the current passage. Two lines about the same shape have a 5-second pause.

All authors, original source URLs and licences are recorded centrally in [CREDITS.md](../../../CREDITS.md).

`printer-line-1.wav` through `printer-line-5.wav` are distinct 0.70-second excerpts beginning at 0.15, 4.05, 7.10, 11.10 and 16.10 seconds in the credited Star LC-10 recording. Delivery format is mono 22,050 Hz, 16-bit PCM WAV. Each uses a 120 Hz high-pass, 8 kHz low-pass, 8 ms fade in, 15 ms fade out and a matched peak of 0.55. The playback gain is 0.035 before spatial and master-volume attenuation. Shuffled sets use every variant and avoid adjacent repeats; the cue fires at a printed line's start rather than repeatedly every frame. Printing and paper feed stop while idle.

`door-open.wav` is 00:00.05–00:01.15 and `door-close.wav` is 00:30.00–00:30.85 of the credited metal-door recording. Both are mono 22,050 Hz, 16-bit PCM WAV, high-passed at 90 Hz, peak-matched to 0.55, with 8 ms fade in and 20 ms fade out. Door playback gain is 0.03 before the master volume, reduced from 0.2 following the owner's loudness feedback. They are hinge textures, not separate original recordings of the game's doors.

`button-press.wav` is mono 22,050 Hz PCM16, 0.14 seconds, a mechanical press isolated from a cassette-key recording with an 80 Hz high-pass, 4,500 Hz low-pass and 5/10 ms fades; playback gain is 0.07 before spatial and master attenuation.

`switch-click.wav` and `rocker-click.wav` are mono 22,050 Hz PCM16 excerpts of the credited StevenBrown rocker recording, 0.15 and 0.20 seconds respectively. All three control recordings are peak-normalised to 0.55. Defaults use gain 0.07 for buttons/switches and 0.08 for rockers, before spatial/master attenuation. Each part sets its own gain beside its sound.

Rotary detents share the crisp switch recording at gain 0.025; selectors use gain 0.06. There is no central table of control sounds: each part in `src/rendering/parts.ts` defines its own sounds beside it (a recording with its gain, or a synthesised click from `src/audio/part-audio.ts`), so a part sounds the same wherever it is placed.

`key-thock.wav` is the second press/release pair from the MattRuthSound mechanical-keyboard recording, filtered and played at 88% source speed during editing, about 0.25 seconds long. Peak 0.55; keycap playback gain 0.09. Source and licence are in CREDITS.md.

`bakelite-heavy-switch.wav` is a 0.42-second excerpt beginning at 1.70 seconds in the owner-supplied Stanwood Studios video. `fluorescent-ballast-start.wav` is a 1.38-second excerpt beginning at 2.17 seconds. Both are mono 22,050 Hz PCM16 WAV with short fades; the startup is raised 18 dB because it is quiet in the source. Their recording credit and source status are in CREDITS.md.

`voice-alarm/*.mp3` are dev8's voice alarm messages (alert, evacuate, false alarm, all clear), synthesised with Piper's British VCTK voice, speaker p226 (`-s 95` in the model's speaker map), at length scale 1.12 and sentence silence 0.45 s, then given a public-address colour with ffmpeg: `highpass=f=220,lowpass=f=6500,aecho=0.85:0.6:45|95:0.22|0.12,loudnorm=I=-18:TP=-2:LRA=7`. The unprocessed renders are in `resources/sound/narrations/voice-alarm/`. The voice alarm part plays them at gain 0.9 from its ceiling speaker; credit in CREDITS.md.

`trolley-squeak.wav` is an 8-second loop from 3.65–11.85 s of the credited shopping-cart HQ MP3 preview. The working original, source/licence record and filtered WAV are in `resources/sound/effects/trolley/`. Processing: mono 22,050 Hz PCM16, 100 Hz high-pass, 7 kHz low-pass, loudnorm targeting -22 LUFS / -3 dBTP / LRA 7, then a 200 ms tail-to-head crossfade. CASTORS plays the loop at gain 0.2 while the stand translates or turns, through the shared positional audio output; its panner follows the moving stand. Deferred loading is cancelled if movement ends before the file is ready.
